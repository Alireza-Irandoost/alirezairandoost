About Me

I am a researcher specializing in MEMS and Micro/Nano devices, with experience in device fabrication, photonics, and biosensors. I have a strong background in electrical engineering and microelectronic device design, and I aim to pursue advanced research in integrated photonics and microsystems.

Projects

  • Neural Probe Design: Developed a flexible neural probe for brain signal recording based on microelectrodes.
  • Widefield Fluorescence Microscope: Designed and simulated a microscope using commercial lenses in Zemax.
  • MEMS Device Fabrication: Worked on micro/nano-fabrication techniques for sensors and actuators.

Publications

  • Irandoost et al., "A Novel Flexible Neural Probe for Brain Signal Recording", IEEE TBME, 2025.
  • Irandoost et al., "Simulation of Widefield Fluorescence Microscopy Using Zemax", 2024.
